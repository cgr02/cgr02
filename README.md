## 📂 Projects

### 🔵 Credit Risk Classification · `IA Course`
Binary classification on Kaggle's Credit Risk Dataset (~29k records).  
Covers data cleaning, median imputation, encoding, and comparison of **Decision Tree / Random Forest / SVM** with KFold cross-validation.  
`pandas` `scikit-learn` `KFold` `classification_report`

---

### 🟢 Social Media & Stress · `Statistics 2`
Linear regression built **from scratch** (no sklearn) on ~399 filtered observations.  
Analyzes the relationship between daily Instagram usage and perceived stress in rural Canadian women under 35.  
Includes SSE, R², t-stat, p-value, residual diagnostics, and non-linear model comparison.  
`pandas` `scipy` `matplotlib` `numpy`

---

### 🟠 Deep Learning with Keras · `AI Course`
Dense neural networks for both **classification** (penguin species) and **regression** (flipper length, supermarket sales).  
Architecture: ReLU hidden layers, sigmoid/linear output, SGD optimizer. Fixed seed: `tf.random.set_seed(21)`.  
`TensorFlow` `Keras` `SGD` `Dense`
